Why This Product Exists

The problem is having neither infrastructure nor a strategy. Complete exists to provide both in one purchase. It combines the Buildbook and the strategy packs of the same date.

What This Product Is

Starter plus the Volume Pack and the Profit Pack of 2026-10.

Complete is those three pieces of one date. It is not a higher edition. It adds no file of its own and no repository of its own. It does not add an execution function. The execution base stays the public platform.

The Volume Pack in this purchase is the 2026-10 set selected for trading volume, venue participation, and point-program opportunity, in live operation on Arcus. The Profit Pack in this purchase is the 2026-10 set selected for net result, in live operation on MEXC Spot and MEXC Futures. A later date is not included. Past results do not guarantee future points, rewards, profit, or an airdrop.

What You Receive

GitHub access is read access to the Starter repository and to both 2026-10 pack repositories. There is no Complete repository. The Discord role does not grant the repositories. The grants happen after the purchase is confirmed. This page does not set how long that takes.

What You Need

  • A TradingView account. A TradingView subscription is required for practical use. Alert limits depend on the TradingView plan.
  • An account at a venue the public release can execute: MEXC Spot, MEXC Futures, or Arcus.

  • Infrastructure you run. The Buildbook in this purchase is the reference deployment. You remain responsible for that infrastructure.
