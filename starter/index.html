This Product Solves

You have a TradingView strategy. Building the infrastructure is difficult.

Why This Product Exists

The code is already public. The problem is not access to the platform. The problem is connecting alerts, execution, and operational records inside infrastructure you run. Starter exists to provide the Buildbook for that process.

What This Product Is

The Buildbook and the operator guidance in that playbook.

Starter is not a support contract and not a second program. The reader follows the process. That takes the ability to read it, the ability to carry out the steps, and patience. Starter includes no strategy set.

The public platform is free. You can deploy it from the public repository without buying a product.

What You Receive

GitHub access is read access to the Starter repository. The Discord role does not grant that access. Both grants happen after the purchase is confirmed. This page does not set how long that takes.

What You Do Not Receive

  • Managed trading
  • Investment advice
  • A profit guarantee
  • A later edition of a pack
  • Future rewards
  • A future airdrop
  • Custody of funds
  • Custody of API keys
  • A support contract

What You Need

  • A TradingView account. A TradingView subscription is required for practical use. Alert limits depend on the TradingView plan.
  • An account at a venue the public release can execute: MEXC Spot, MEXC Futures, or Arcus.
  • API credentials you create at that venue.
  • Infrastructure you run. Starter's Buildbook is the reference deployment. You remain responsible for that infrastructure.
