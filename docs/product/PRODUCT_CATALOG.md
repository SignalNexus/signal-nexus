# Signal Nexus Product Catalog

This file is the comparison source. It is not a sales page, and it does not take payment. GitHub Pages does not use this file as its own step. The home page [index.md](../../index.md) carries this comparison as its last section. Read More on that page opens the Gumroad product page.

What each product is, is defined in [Product Specification](PRODUCT_SPECIFICATION.md). The Japanese edition of this page is [PRODUCT_CATALOG_JA.md](PRODUCT_CATALOG_JA.md).

## Overview

Signal Nexus is a research-to-execution platform you run on your own infrastructure. Funds stay at the venue. Keys stay on infrastructure you run. Signal Nexus does not custody assets.

The public platform is Free. You can deploy it from this repository without buying a product. Free is not one of the four rows below.

The four products on this page are independent. You choose by the problem you are solving. They are not a ladder, and Complete is not a higher edition of another product.

VenueAdd is an extension for one venue that is not in this public release. It is not in the comparison below. Its definition is in the Product Specification.

Nothing on this page promises profit, points, an airdrop, or a trading result.

## Product Lineup

- Starter. The public platform, plus the Buildbook. The Buildbook is the playbook for the reference deployment. Starter is not a second program, and it does not include a support contract.
- Volume Pack. The strategy set of one purchase date, selected with trading volume, venue participation, and point-program opportunity as the axis. The selection uses past data, backtests, and past operation, including operation the author has run in public. You run the set. It is not a research report. It does not include a later date. Past results do not guarantee future points, rewards, profit, or an airdrop.
- Profit Pack. The strategy set of one purchase date, selected with net result as the axis. The selection uses past data, backtests, and past operation, including operation the author has run in public. You run the set. It is not a research report. It does not include a later date. Past results do not guarantee future profit.
- Complete. Starter together with the Volume Pack and the Profit Pack of the same date. It does not add a separate support contract. It is not a higher edition of either pack. There is no Complete repository.

## Which Product Is Right For You?

Choose by the problem you are solving. The headings below are not a ranking, and they are not a description of who you are.

### You have a TradingView strategy. Building the infrastructure is difficult.

Starter.

The public platform is already Free. Starter is the Buildbook and the operator guidance for reaching TradingView, execution, and analytics on infrastructure you run. It does not include a strategy set.

### You can build the infrastructure. You do not have an effective strategy.

Profit Pack, Volume Pack, or both.

The two packs are independent. Buying one is not a condition for buying the other. Each is a strategy set of one purchase date, not a research report. Profit Pack is selected for net result. Volume Pack is selected for trading volume, venue participation, and point-program opportunity. Selection uses past data, backtests, and past operation, including operation the author has run in public. Neither includes the Buildbook. Neither includes a later date. Past results do not guarantee future profit, points, rewards, or an airdrop.

### You have neither the infrastructure nor a strategy.

Complete.

Complete is Starter together with the Volume Pack and the Profit Pack of the same date, in one purchase. It is that bundle. It is not a higher edition, and it does not include VenueAdd.

## FAQ

### Is this open-source?

No. The public repository is source-available. You may read it and run it for your own personal use. Commercial use is not granted. The author retains the rights. The terms are in [LICENSE](../../LICENSE.md).

### What do I receive after purchase?

The row in the comparison table for the product you bought. Starter and Complete include the Buildbook. Volume Pack and Profit Pack include that date's strategy set. Complete includes both sets of that same date. The sets do not promise a trading result, points, rewards, or an airdrop.

### How is access granted?

After the purchase is confirmed, a GitHub Team grants read access to the repository for that product. A Discord role grants the purchaser community. The Discord role does not grant the repository. This page does not grant either.

### How long does access provisioning take?

This page does not set a duration. Provisioning is complete when the GitHub Team and the Discord role are both in place. The sales page describes how the current purchase is handled.

### Can I purchase another product later?

Yes. A later purchase is another independent product. It does not convert the product you already have. A pack stays the strategy set of its purchase date.
