Why This Product Exists

The problem is not infrastructure. The problem is selecting strategies. Profit Pack exists to provide the strategy set selected on net result. It is a strategy pack, not a research report.

What This Product Is

The strategy set of purchase date 2026-10. A later date is not included. It is not a research report.

Volume Pack and Profit Pack use the same set of files. The strategy set inside them differs. Profit Pack is the set in live operation on MEXC Spot and MEXC Futures. The files are README.md, strategy-catalog.md, profit-analysis.md, tradingview-setup-guide.md, sample yml, pine-runtime-spec, and pine/. profit-analysis.md stays separate from the catalog.

This pack does not include the Buildbook. Selection uses past data, backtests, and past operation, including operation the author has run in public. Past results, including a win rate, do not guarantee future profit.
